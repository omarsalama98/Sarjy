S5 -- browser audio spike

Throwaway diagnostic page, not part of the app. Marked delete-after-Block-2. See docs/plans/blocks/00-spikes.md S5 for the five checks (C1-C5) this exercises. Serve with npm run dev from frontend/, open http://localhost:5173/spike.html.

mic track sampleRate: -  |  AudioContext state: -  |  current state: idle

Section A -- capture -> 16kHz PCM16 -> playback round trip

C1 (Chrome) / C2 (Safari): click Start, say a full sentence out loud, click Stop, then Play. Can you understand your own sentence played back? Also download the .wav and save it as scratch/spikes/out/s5-roundtrip.wav -- a second listener should be able to transcribe it.

C5 is covered for free here: watch the "AudioContext state" indicator above -- it should flip from nothing to running the instant you click Start (that click is the required user gesture). If it ever shows suspended after a click, that is the bug C5 exists to catch.

Section B -- echo cancellation loopback (use SPEAKERS, not headphones)

C3: click Run. It plays a 2s tone through your speakers while recording from the mic at the same time. Download the clip and listen: if echo cancellation is working, the tone should NOT be clearly audible in the capture (it cancels what the browser itself just played). Save as scratch/spikes/out/s5-echo-test.wav.

Headphones defeat this test by construction -- the mic can't hear speakers that aren't there.

Section C -- VAD (speech start/end timing)

C4: click Start, then say a full sentence -- watch the log for onSpeechStart / onSpeechEnd and the measured delay. Then try saying just "mhm" or "yeah" -- it should log onVADMisfire, NOT a full onSpeechEnd (a misfire means the backchannel correctly did NOT end the turn). If a short backchannel DOES fire a full speech-end, that's the failure C4 is checking for at this threshold.
